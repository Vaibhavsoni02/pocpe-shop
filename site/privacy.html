Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. This policy explains what personal data shop.pocpe.com collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

pocpe (the "shop", "we", "us") runs shop.pocpe.com and decides how and why your personal data is used (the "controller"). pocpe is not currently a registered company, so the best way to reach us is by email: vaibhav@pocpe.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer; that address handles all privacy questions.

The shop is currently in demonstration mode. Checkout takes no payment and nothing is shipped, and we do not yet keep an order history; the order details above apply once orders are live. When we start delivering orders we will share your name, delivery address and phone number with our delivery partners, and name them in this policy. We use it in Google Analytics and Mixpanel to connect your visits and orders. If you withdraw analytics consent, we delete it from your browser.

5. Transfers outside your country

Our providers process data in several countries, including India and the United States. Where the law requires a safeguard for sending personal data outside the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we use the mechanisms available under each provider's terms, such as adequacy decisions (for example the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients) or standard contractual clauses with the UK addendum. You can ask us about the safeguards at vaibhav@pocpe.com.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or move it to another service. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting what happened before: use "Cookie settings" for cookies, and the Email preferences page for email. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Write to vaibhav@pocpe.com; we aim to answer within one month. We may need to confirm who you are first.

When you ask us to delete your email profile we also delete the cookie-consent history linked to it, and we will ask Google, Mixpanel and Meta to delete data linked to you where we are able to identify it.

8. Security

The site uses HTTPS and standard security headers, preference changes require a signed link, and access to our systems is limited. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The shop is not aimed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes and contact

We will post changes here and, where required, ask for your consent again. Questions or requests: vaibhav@pocpe.com.
